    VPN

    The built-in VPN uses Cloudflare WARP’s free WireGuard network. No account, no log-in, and NoamBrowser runs no servers of its own.

    Off
    Connection type
    Proxy addresssocks5://, http:// or https://. Add user:pass@ if your provider needs it.
    Kill switchIf the VPN drops, block traffic instead of going direct.
    Connect when NoamBrowser starts
    New identityGenerates fresh WireGuard keys and a new WARP device.

    WARP hides your IP address from websites and encrypts traffic between you and Cloudflare. Your exit is usually in your own country, so it won’t unlock other regions. For a specific country, use your own proxy.

    Site permissions

      Translate

      Translation runs entirely on this computer using the open Firefox Translations engine. Pages are never sent to Google or anyone else.

      Offer to translate pages in other languagesShows a translate button in the address bar.
      Translate into

      Each language model downloads the first time you use it (about 15–40 MB) and then works offline. Languages without a direct model are translated through English.

      Import

      Bring your bookmarks and history over from another browser on this computer. Nothing in the other browser is changed.

      Looking for other browsers…

      Passwords: browsers lock their saved passwords, so export them first — in Chrome, Edge or Brave open Settings → Passwords → Export passwords — then use Import from CSV.

      Sync

      Keep bookmarks, settings, your reading list, site notes, to-dos, saved sessions and (optionally) passwords the same on all your computers — Windows, Mac and Linux. No account and no NoamBrowser server: it syncs through a folder your cloud drive already syncs (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive) and everything is encrypted with your passphrase first.

      Sync folderPick a folder inside OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox or iCloud Drive.
      Sync passphraseUse the same passphrase on every computer. Write it down — it can’t be recovered.
      Also sync saved passwordsEncrypted with your passphrase before they’re written to the folder.
