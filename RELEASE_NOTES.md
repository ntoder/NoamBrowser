# NoamBrowser 3.2 ⭐ Ready to be your main browser

## 🎬 Netflix, Spotify, Disney+ and other streaming
NoamBrowser now plays protected video (Widevine). The first time you open NoamBrowser it downloads a small video module from Google in the background.

## 🖥️ Share your screen in video calls
"Share screen" in Google Meet, Zoom, Discord, Teams and others now works. NoamBrowser shows a picker with live previews of your screens and windows. On Windows you can also share your computer's sound.

## ⭐ Make it your default browser
- **Settings → General → Make default** now really works on Windows, Mac and Linux. On Windows, NoamBrowser now appears in Windows' list of browsers.
- NoamBrowser asks you once (you can say "Not now" or "Don't ask again").

## 📚 Chrome bookmarks, exactly as they were
- Importing from Chrome keeps your **bookmarks bar, folders, folders inside folders and the order**.
- "Other bookmarks" and "Mobile bookmarks" become their own folders, like in Chrome.
- New **Replace my NoamBrowser bookmarks** switch (Settings → Import): your bookmarks become exactly Chrome's, and the old ones are saved to a backup file first.
- The bookmarks bar can mix folders and bookmarks in any order. Folders inside folders open in place, with a ‹ back button. Right-click a folder → **New folder inside…**

## 🔄 Sync fixes
- The same link in two folders is no longer merged into one.
- Moving a bookmark to another folder now syncs to your other computers.
- The order of your bookmarks bar syncs too.

## 🧰 Everyday fixes
- Pop-up boxes that ask you to type something (`prompt()`) now work.
- **"Leave site? Changes you made may not be saved"** now appears when a page has unsaved work.
- **micro:bit, Arduino and other USB / serial devices** can connect from web editors such as MakeCode.

Already have NoamBrowser? It updates itself. New install: https://noamtoder.com/get/noambrowser
