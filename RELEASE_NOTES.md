# NoamBrowser 3.3 👋 Switching from Chrome or Firefox is easy now

## 👋 New: the Switch guide
A step-by-step guide that brings over everything from your old browser in about a minute. It opens by itself when you first install NoamBrowser. You can open it again any time from **Menu → Switch from Chrome or Firefox…** or **Settings → Import**.
1. **Pick your old browser.** NoamBrowser finds Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Firefox, Waterfox, LibreWolf and Zen on your computer.
2. **Choose what to bring:** bookmarks, history, search engine, home page and the tabs you had open.
3. **Passwords:** clear steps for exporting them from Chrome or Firefox and importing them here.
4. **Extensions:** your Chrome extensions can be added with one click. For your Firefox add-ons, it finds the Chrome version. It also tells you which ones you don't need anymore because NoamBrowser already does what they did (uBlock Origin, Dark Reader, Containers, ClearURLs, cookie-banner blockers and more).
5. **Make it yours:** make NoamBrowser your default browser, turn on Simple look, show the bookmarks bar and reopen your tabs.
6. **Where's everything:** a cheat sheet showing where the things you're used to are in NoamBrowser.

## 🦊 Firefox import
- Bookmarks, with the **Bookmarks Toolbar** going to your bookmarks bar in the same order, plus the Bookmarks Menu, Other Bookmarks and folders inside folders.
- History, so the address bar suggests the sites you visit and new tabs show your most-visited sites.
- Your search engine, home page and open tabs.
- Also works with Waterfox, LibreWolf and Zen, and with Snap and Flatpak installs on Linux.

## 🟡 More from Chrome (and Edge, Brave…)
Your search engine, home page, "continue where you left off" setting and extensions now come over too, not just bookmarks and history.

## ⌨️ The shortcuts you already know
- **Ctrl+Shift+P**: private window (Firefox)
- **Ctrl+Shift+O**: bookmarks manager
- **Ctrl+Shift+Delete**: clear browsing data
- **Ctrl+Shift+I / J / C**: developer tools
- **Ctrl+Shift+H**: history (Firefox)
- **Ctrl+Shift+Y**: downloads (Firefox)
- **Ctrl+Shift+W**: close window
- **Alt+F** or **Alt+E**: open the menu (Windows and Linux)
- To open a folder, use **Ctrl+Alt+O** (Ctrl+Shift+O now opens bookmarks, like in Chrome).

## 🧘 Simple look
A new switch in **Settings → General** (and in the Switch guide) hides the extra toolbar buttons and the side bar, so NoamBrowser looks and feels like Chrome or Firefox. Everything is still there. Turn it off to get the buttons back.
